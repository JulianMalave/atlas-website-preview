Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Know what to expect before you get connected.

Is there a fee to work with Atlas?

No. Atlas offers free, no-obligation consultations and is compensated by participating providers.

Do you help both businesses and homeowners?

Yes. We help businesses and residential customers explore eligible connectivity solutions.

Can you help me switch from my current provider?

Yes. We can help compare options and coordinate eligible new orders or transitions.

Can you guarantee a lower price?

No. Pricing, eligibility, and availability vary by provider and address. If your current setup is the better fit, we'll tell you.

Where does Atlas provide service?

Atlas is based in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, and works across multiple markets, depending on provider availability.

What happens if I need help after activation?

Atlas can help with provider coordination and account questions. Network repairs and carrier technical support remain with the provider.
