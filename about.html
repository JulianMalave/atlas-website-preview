WHY ATLAS EXISTS

You shouldn't have to call five companies to find one good solution.

Internet, wireless and business technology are essential, but shopping for them can be complicated. Comparing providers means sorting through pricing, promotions, service differences and contract terms.

For busy business owners and households, that process takes time they often don't have. Atlas was created to help carry that burden by making the available options easier to understand and compare.

OUR MISSION

Make getting connected easier, more affordable and less complicated.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Built to fill a gap in the industry.

Atlas Network Solutions was founded by Julian Malave, a telecommunications professional based in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, who recognized a significant gap in the industry: the need for a customer-first representative who could look beyond a single provider and help customers make informed decisions.

Throughout his experience in telecommunications, Julian encountered business owners who were overpaying for outdated services, locked into unfavorable agreements or simply unaware that better options existed.

The problem wasn't always a lack of alternatives. It was the time and effort required to find them.

Business owners were already busy running their companies. They didn't have hours to spend contacting multiple providers, comparing pricing, reviewing contract terms and navigating complicated promotions.

Julian saw firsthand how much of a burden that process had become. More importantly, he saw the relief it brought when someone could simplify those decisions, identify opportunities to save money and help businesses upgrade their technology without the usual headaches.

That experience became the foundation of Atlas Network Solutions.

Atlas was built around a different approach to telecommunications: putting customers' needs first, comparing solutions across participating providers and recommending options based on value rather than allegiance to any single carrier.

What began as a way to better serve business owners has grown into a mission to help both businesses and households make smarter, more confident connectivity decisions.
