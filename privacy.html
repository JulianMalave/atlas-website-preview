Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Atlas Network Solutions values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you when you visit our website, submit a form, request a consultation, or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your business location
  • Information about your current internet, phone, wireless, backup connectivity, security, or entertainment services
  • Information you include in consultation requests, forms, messages, or other communications

We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as your browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location, and interaction data.

2. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected through:

  • Our website
  • Consultation and quote-request forms
  • Meta or Facebook lead advertisements
  • Tally forms
  • Email, telephone, text message, or live chat
  • Analytics and advertising technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions or consultation request
  • Review your current business technology services
  • Prepare recommendations, comparisons, or quotes
  • Help identify available internet, phone, wireless, backup, security, or entertainment solutions
  • Coordinate service orders, installations, activations, or provider communications
  • Provide customer service and follow-up
  • Improve our website, advertising, and services
  • Send service-related communications you have requested or agreed to receive
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Information Sharing

Atlas Network Solutions does not sell your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary with telecommunications carriers, internet providers, wireless providers, VoIP providers, security providers, commercial entertainment providers, technology partners, installation partners, or other service providers in order to prepare quotes or provide services you request.

We may also share information with vendors that help us operate our website, forms, customer communications, analytics, advertising, scheduling, or customer relationship management systems.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, customers, business, or the public.

5. Meta and Facebook Lead Ads

When you submit information through a Meta or Facebook lead advertisement, the information you provide may be sent to Atlas Network Solutions and used to respond to your request, provide information, schedule a consultation, or discuss relevant products and services.

Meta may process information according to its own privacy policies and platform terms. Atlas Network Solutions does not control Meta's independent collection or use of information.

6. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website activity, measure advertising performance, improve user experience, and evaluate how visitors interact with our pages and forms.

These technologies may be provided by third parties, including Meta. You can manage certain cookie or tracking preferences through your browser or device settings.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to or use third-party services, including Tally, Meta, Google, scheduling platforms, telecommunications providers, and customer communication tools.

Those third parties maintain their own privacy practices. Atlas Network Solutions is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites or platforms.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also ask us to stop sending marketing communications. Service-related or legally required communications may still be sent when appropriate.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
